# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful learning community where people can share knowledge and improve educational material regardless of experience, background, identity, or circumstance.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, constructive, and specific.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Critique ideas and contributions without attacking people.
- Credit the work of others and correct attribution mistakes promptly.
- Accept review in good faith and focus discussion on learners' needs.
- Protect private information and report security concerns responsibly.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Deliberate misinformation, plagiarism, spam, or deceptive promotion.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Malicious code, unsafe instructions presented deceptively, or attempts to disrupt the project.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, discussions, reviews, contributions, community spaces, and public interactions made on behalf of the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers when a private channel is available, or use the repository's moderation and reporting tools. Do not publicly disclose sensitive details about the affected person.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other participation that violates this policy. Responses may include a correction request, warning, temporary restriction, or permanent removal from project spaces, depending on context and severity.

Maintainers should handle reports fairly, minimize unnecessary disclosure, avoid conflicts of interest, and document significant moderation decisions where doing so is safe.

## Stewardship

This policy may evolve through transparent, reviewable repository changes as the community grows.
